About

Why the firm is called Provenance.

In the art world, provenance is the documented history of an object — where it came from, whose hands it passed through, and the papers that prove it. A painting with a gap in its provenance is hard to sell, however fine it looks.

The idea

Customers ask for the documented history of your data and AI.

A product can work beautifully and still be hard to sell, if no one can show where its data came from or how its AI was built and checked.

A buyer who cannot check takes the risk on themselves. A company that cannot show where its data and AI came from will struggle when a customer, an auditor or a regulator asks. Provenance is being set up to close that gap, with documents.

The founder

Hemant Patil

Hemant Patil leads Provenance, combining three things that compliance work usually splits across three different people: engineering, commercial experience and legal training. EU laws such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act set requirements for how a product is designed and built, and meeting them well needs all three skills together.

Engineer
He graduated in engineering. He knows how data moves through a system, and what a change in one place does to the rest.
24 years answering buyers’ compliance questions
At Oracle, IBM and other technology companies, in more than forty countries, customers asked him whether the products and services they were buying met their requirements, and he answered them. Provenance does the same for your data and AI: each answer comes with the document behind it.
Certified in data protection and AI governance
CIPP/E in European data protection law and AIGP in AI governance, two of the most widely recognised certifications in their fields.
Thesis: the GDPR and the AI Act, assessed together
His LL.M. thesis in Data Economy Law, now in its final stage, is about carrying out the GDPR and EU AI Act risk assessments together.

He is a qualified lawyer, although not admitted to any Bar in the EU, and Provenance is not a law firm. Provenance’s job is to find the facts, prepare the documents that prove them and keep them up to date, and that is what he is trained and certified to do. If a step needs a lawyer, such as writing a contract or going to court, your own lawyer takes it, with everything already prepared.

Working with Provenance

What you can expect.

  • Same person, three roles, one fixed fee. The person you first speak to is the person who does the work, and he brings the engineering, the commercial experience and the legal training it needs. You pay one fixed fee, agreed in writing before work starts. No hand-off to a junior team.
  • How the work is done. AI does the routine work, and Hemant checks every point that matters before it reaches you. That is part of how one person can deliver this work for one fixed fee. No personal data goes into any AI tool unless you have approved a written data sharing agreement that covers it.
  • The same person speaks the language of sales, engineers and lawyers. Your sales lead, your engineers and your lawyers each get the answer in language and terms they are familiar with.
  • No answer without a document behind it. Provenance does not call anything a fact without a document, record or number that shows it. Where something is not yet in place, you are told, with a date by which it can be in place. A point that needs a legal decision goes straight on the list for your lawyers.
