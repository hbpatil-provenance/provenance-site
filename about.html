About

Why it is called Provenance.

In the art world, provenance is the documented history of an object — where it came from, whose hands it passed through, and the papers that prove it. A painting with a gap in its provenance is hard to sell, however fine it looks.

The idea

Data and AI have reached the same point.

A product can work beautifully and still be hard to sell, if no one can show where its data came from or how its AI was built and checked.

A buyer who cannot check is taking a risk under their own name. A company that cannot show its own trail will struggle the day a customer, an auditor or a regulator asks. Provenance is being set up to close that gap, with documents.

Working with Provenance

What you can expect.

  • One senior person, start to finish. The person you first write to does the work.
  • The same person speaks the language of sales, engineers and lawyers. Your sales lead, your engineers and your lawyers each get the answer in terms they use.
  • Plain limits. If something cannot be shown, or is a question for a lawyer, you are told at once.

Where things stand

Not open yet.

Provenance is being established in the EU as a Portuguese company and expects to begin work in 2027. Until then it takes on no engagements and charges no fees. It is one senior person today, and it will say so plainly when its calendar is full.

Being established in the Union is a deliberate choice. Some of the roles Provenance plans to take, such as representing a company from outside Europe, are open only to a firm established in the Union. The firm will work in English, with clients across Europe and beyond.

Everything on this website describes what Provenance plans to offer once it opens. The fees shown are a guide and may change before then.
