Partner with Provenance

Add EU compliance to your team — for as long as you need it.

Not every company needs a compliance hire, and not every law firm wants to staff the operational side of data and AI work. Provenance plugs in: inside a product launch, behind a law firm, or as a standing partner that helps your deals clear compliance and reach signature faster.

01 · For teams building something new

Launching, with no one to own compliance.

You are bringing an AI or data product to market, and the questions are stacking up — what the AI Act asks, where the data can go, what your first customers will want to see. Nobody inside owns them yet.

Provenance joins the project as your compliance and governance capability, from the early design decisions through to the evidence your first customers ask for — then hands it to your team when you are ready to run it yourselves.

What that looks like

  • An embedded compliance lead for the launch
  • The assessments, policies and evidence the product needs
  • A team that can carry it once you go live

02 · For law firms

Capacity for the work you would rather not staff.

Your clients want the operational side of GDPR, AI Act or DORA work — registers, assessments, evidence packs, questionnaire answers — the kind of delivery that is hard to resource inside a firm.

Provenance does that work behind you, to your brief. You keep the client and every legal call; your client gets the whole job finished. The line stays clean: Provenance establishes the facts and builds the controls, and your firm gives the advice.

What that looks like

  • The operational compliance work, delivered under your direction
  • A clear boundary — Provenance never gives legal advice
  • One senior point of contact, on demand

03 · For companies that need a result, not a hire

Fractional, and paid by the outcome.

Sometimes you need one specific thing done well, by someone senior — not another person on the payroll.

Hire Provenance for a defined result: a transfer file assembled, an AI Act assessment completed, an EU representative stood up. Scoped, priced and delivered, then it stops until the next outcome needs it. Where a matter needs local law, Provenance brings in locally admitted counsel and specialists per market, so it scales across Europe without you building a team.

What that looks like

  • A named senior specialist for one outcome
  • A fixed scope and price, and no retainer
  • Local counsel and specialists brought in when a market needs them

04 · For law firms and technology companies

A standing partner that helps deals reach signature.

You have a steady flow of EU deals, and the same thing slows each one: the data and AI questions between proposal and signature.

Provenance handles the recurring data and AI questions that stall your deals, to an agreed turnaround — and brings in extra specialists when volume needs it, so one senior contact never becomes the bottleneck. Over time it learns your product and your customers’ questions, and the compliance step stops being the thing that holds a deal up.

What that looks like

  • A repeatable way to clear the compliance part of every deal
  • Shorter proposal-to-contract cycles
  • A partner who knows your product and your market

How it stays simple

One clear line, every time.

In every one of these, Provenance does the compliance and governance work — the facts, the controls, the evidence. The law stays with the lawyers: your in-house team, your firm, or counsel Provenance brings in.

That is what keeps a partnership easy to say yes to, and easy to explain to your own clients.
