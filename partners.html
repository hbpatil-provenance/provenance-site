Four ways to work with Provenance

Provenance does the compliance work. Your lawyers make every legal decision.

This is the same whether Provenance joins your product launch, works with your law firm, does a single job, or helps with the questions that come up in your EU deals.

For teams building something new

Get a compliance specialist working with your team from the start of your launch.

You are bringing an AI or data product to market, and the open questions are growing: what the AI Act asks, where the data can go, what your first customers will want to see. No one in your company is responsible for them yet.

Provenance joins the project to handle compliance and governance, from the early design decisions to the evidence your first customers ask for. Then it hands the work to your team when you are ready to run it yourselves.

What that looks like

  • A compliance lead working with your team on the launch
  • The assessments, policies and evidence the product needs
  • A handover, so your own team can run it once you go live

For law firms

Provenance does the compliance work for your clients. You stay their lawyer.

Your clients want the operational side of GDPR, AI Act or DORA work: registers, assessments, evidence packs, questionnaire answers. This kind of delivery is hard to staff inside a firm, so the client takes it elsewhere.

Provenance does that work for your firm, under your direction. The client stays yours, every legal decision stays with you, and the client gets the whole job done. The roles stay separate: Provenance establishes the facts and builds the controls, and your firm gives the advice.

What that looks like

  • The operational compliance work, delivered under your direction
  • A clear boundary — Provenance never gives legal advice
  • One senior point of contact, on demand
  • Take on the whole client project without hiring more people

For companies that need a result, not a hire

Get one specific job done by a senior specialist, without hiring.

Sometimes you need one specific thing done well by someone senior, without hiring another employee.

Engage Provenance for one defined result: a transfer file assembled, an AI Act assessment completed, an EU representative in place. It is scoped and priced before it starts, and it ends when the result is delivered.

Where a matter needs advice on local law, you use your own counsel, or Provenance introduces a locally bar-registered lawyer at no cost, for you to assess independently.

What that looks like

  • A named senior specialist for one result
  • A fixed scope and fee, agreed first
  • Your own counsel, or an introduction at no cost, where local law is needed

For law firms and technology companies

Keep EU deals moving, without starting from scratch each time.

You have a steady flow of EU deals, and the same data and AI questions return in each one. Each time, someone on your team rebuilds the answers.

Provenance keeps one evidence pack for your product and updates it as the product changes. Every new customer’s questions start from answers already written and already backed by documents. Your sales team replies with confidence, and says the same thing each time.

What that looks like

  • One maintained evidence pack for your product
  • Less repeated work on each new deal
  • A partner who knows your product and your customers’ questions

How it stays simple

What Provenance does, and what your lawyers do.

In every one of these, Provenance does the compliance and governance work — the facts, the controls, the evidence. The law stays with the lawyers: your in-house team, your firm, or a locally bar-registered lawyer Provenance introduces at no cost.

That is what keeps a partnership easy to say yes to, and easy to explain to your own clients.
