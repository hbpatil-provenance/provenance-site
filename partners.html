Product teams · Law firms · One result · Repeat deals

Provenance does the compliance work. Your lawyers make every legal decision.

That line doesn't move, whether Provenance sits inside your product launch, behind your law firm, on one defined result, or as the standing partner on the questions that return in every EU deal.

01 · For teams building something new

Launch with compliance owned from the first day.

You are bringing an AI or data product to market, and the questions are stacking up — what the AI Act asks, where the data can go, what your first customers will want to see. Nobody inside owns them yet.

Provenance joins the project as your compliance and governance capability, from the early design decisions through to the evidence your first customers ask for — then hands it to your team when you are ready to run it yourselves.

What that looks like

  • An embedded compliance lead for the launch
  • The assessments, policies and evidence the product needs
  • A team that can carry it once you go live

02 · For law firms

Offer your client the whole job. Keep the client and every legal call.

Your clients want the operational side of GDPR, AI Act or DORA work — registers, assessments, evidence packs, questionnaire answers — the kind of delivery that is hard to resource inside a firm.

Provenance does that work behind you, to your brief. You keep the client and every legal call; your client gets the whole job finished. The line stays clean: Provenance establishes the facts and builds the controls, and your firm gives the advice.

What that looks like

  • The operational compliance work, delivered under your direction
  • A clear boundary — Provenance never gives legal advice
  • One senior point of contact, on demand

03 · For companies that need a result, not a hire

One result, from a senior person. No new headcount.

Sometimes you need one specific thing done well, by someone senior — not another person on the payroll.

Engage Provenance for one defined result: a transfer file assembled, an AI Act assessment completed, an EU representative in place. It is scoped and priced before it starts, and it ends when the result is delivered.

Where a matter needs local law, you use your own counsel, or Provenance introduces a locally bar-registered lawyer at no cost, for you to assess independently.

What that looks like

  • A named senior specialist for one result
  • A fixed scope and fee, agreed first
  • Your own counsel, or an introduction at no cost, where local law is needed

04 · For law firms and technology companies

Keep EU deals moving, without starting from scratch each time.

You have a steady flow of EU deals, and the same data and AI questions return in each one. Each time, someone on your team rebuilds the answers.

Provenance keeps one evidence pack for your product and updates it as the product changes. Every new customer’s questions start from answers already written and already proven. Your sales team replies with confidence, and says the same thing each time.

What that looks like

  • One maintained evidence pack for your product
  • Less repeated work on each new deal
  • A partner who knows your product and your customers’ questions

How it stays simple

One clear line, every time.

In every one of these, Provenance does the compliance and governance work — the facts, the controls, the evidence. The law stays with the lawyers: your in-house team, your firm, or a locally bar-registered lawyer Provenance introduces at no cost.

That is what keeps a partnership easy to say yes to, and easy to explain to your own clients.
